COST
  Fargate bills the *request*, rounded up to the smallest available task
  (0.25 vCPU / 0.5 GB, about $9/pod/month in {{ .Values.global.region }}).
  At these requests, 3 services x 2 replicas is roughly $54/month; scaling to
  4 replicas each would reach roughly $162/month.

  Set a *daily* budget alarm rather than a monthly one. The fixed baseline
  (EKS control plane + NAT gateway + add-on pods) is already about $145/month,
  so a low monthly alarm would fire every single month and teach you to ignore it.
